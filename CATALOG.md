# Catalog

One line per section variant and effect. Read this first, pick, then open only the chosen file. Fingerprints use the vocabulary in `rigal-web-design/references/catalog-vocabulary.md` (layout, headline, density, background, effect, mood) so a client screenshot described in the same words can be matched here. Browse everything rendered at `/kit` (screenshots in `docs/catalog/`).

Every section takes the shared slot props from `src/sections/types.ts`: `eyebrow`, `headline`, `emphasis`, `line`, `proof`, `primary`, `secondary`, `media`, `id`. Heroes also take `kicker` (the GBP style H1 pill; default on), `keyword` (the word in the headline that carries the motion moment; picked from the page data when left out) and `keywordVariant` (`highlighter`, `underline` or `fill`).

## Hero rules (Lou, 2026-11-07)

- **Complete at first paint.** Headline, proof line and buttons are fully visible in the first frame: never opacity 0, never waiting on a script. No reveal in a hero.
- **One keyword moment, CSS only.** The hero's motion is `src/effects/Keyword.astro` on one or two keywords inside the headline, chosen from the service and city the Google Business Profile ranks for (`src/effects/keyword.ts` reads `src/data/site.ts`). The word stays real text in place; the mark animates around it and settles within about 1.5 seconds. Floor one moment, ceiling two (one leading). Reduced motion lands finished. Button hover and press do not count.
- **No GSAP in the hero.** GSAP (`src/effects/gsap-lazy.ts`) loads only when the first `[data-gsap]` section below the hero nears the viewport, after first contentful paint, and skips anything inside `.hero`.
- **Marquee directly below the hero**, as its own band (`marquee/MarqueeBand`), never inside it. It moves further down the page only when Lou says so.
- **Pills are text only.** Eyebrow, kicker and footer pills carry no dot and no icon.
- **Type comes from the preset** in `src/site.config.ts` (`type`: plex, druk or screamer; `src/data/type-presets.ts`). The condensed presets set headlines in capitals and open up the scale through `data-type` on `<html>`.

## Reserved parts of every page

- **Booking slot.** The primary button in every hero, the banner, the Wash CTA and the mobile action bar pill is the booking slot (`src/components/Actions.astro`, `ActionBar.astro`). With `booking.enabled` in `src/site.config.ts` it goes to `/book` and shows the next open windows under it; off, it falls back to `SITE.primaryAction` with no layout change.
- **Front Desk launcher.** `Base.astro` always ships the Front Desk script tag. The launcher mounts bottom right. Nothing of ours sits in that corner: on phones the action bar owns the bottom 82px full width and the launcher is lifted above it (`--fd-offset-bottom` in `global.css`, 96px plus the safe area at 1100px and below; 16px on desktop). Keep floating cards, review nudges and sticky anything out of the bottom right 96 by 96px on desktop and the bottom right 96 by 180px on phones.

## Effects

| Name | File | Fingerprint effect value | Best for | Engine | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keyword | `src/effects/Keyword.astro` (with `KeywordHeadline.astro`, `keyword.ts`) | keyword-highlighter, keyword-underline, keyword-fill | Every hero and banner headline; the only motion allowed in a hero | CSS | One word stays real text in place while a mark animates around it from first paint: a highlighter sweeping behind, an underline drawing beneath, a color fill passing through. Settles in about 1.5 seconds. Floor one, ceiling two per hero. |
| SplitLineReveal | `src/effects/SplitLineReveal.astro` | split-line-reveal | An H2 that opens a key section, below the hero | GSAP SplitText | Lines rise out of their own line masks. The real text stays one node; GSAP animates an aria-hidden copy and the real text returns when it is done. |
| WordStagger | `src/effects/WordStagger.astro` | word-stagger | Ledes, proof lines, short sub headlines below the hero | GSAP SplitText | Words rise and fade in sequence on the aria-hidden copy. |
| MaskReveal | `src/effects/MaskReveal.astro` | mask-reveal | Every photo frame | CSS | Photo slides up inside its frame while settling from 1.12 to 1. Shapes: arch, pitch, chamfer, square, wide. |
| ScribbleUnderline | `src/effects/ScribbleUnderline.astro` | scribble-underline | One or two phrases per page, below the hero | GSAP DrawSVG | SVG stroke draws itself. In a hero use Keyword underline instead. Rotate with the other accents (rigal-design-rules). |
| HighlighterBar | `src/effects/HighlighterBar.astro` | highlighter-bar | A phrase in an H2 below the hero | CSS | Marker sweep grows left to right behind the lower third on scroll into view. |
| OutlinedToSolid | `src/effects/OutlinedToSolid.astro` | outlined-to-solid | The KineticBand; bars, auto | GSAP (clip-path) | Alternating solid and outlined lines; `fill` makes the outlined lines fill left to right once in view (the shape morph). |
| ScrambleText | `src/effects/ScrambleText.astro` | scramble-text | One word or short phrase, once per page, below the hero; bars, auto | GSAP ScrambleText | Characters settle out of a scramble into the real word on an aria-hidden copy; the real text returns when done. |
| WeightShift | `src/effects/WeightShift.astro` | weight-shift | A story or about headline | GSAP ScrollTrigger | Variable font weight rises as the headline climbs (scrubbed). Size box is fixed at the heavy weight. |
| Marquee | `src/effects/Marquee.astro` | marquee | Inside `marquee/MarqueeBand`, directly below the hero | CSS | Looping row with a pause button; static under reduced motion. |
| CounterRoll | `src/effects/CounterRoll.astro` | counter-roll | Real counts only (years, jobs, reviews) | CSS | Odometer digits. Never for invented stats. |
| ScriptAccent | `src/effects/ScriptAccent.astro` | script-accent | Beside a hero headline | CSS | Handwritten note with a small arrow (the preset's script face, or the display face in italic on plex). Accents only, never headings or body. |
| GradientSweep | `src/effects/GradientSweep.astro` | gradient-sweep | One phrase, once per page, below the hero | CSS | Color band sweeps through and settles on a solid color. |

Two engines. **CSS** effects (Keyword, HighlighterBar, MaskReveal, Marquee, CounterRoll, ScriptAccent, GradientSweep) run on keyframes and transitions through `src/effects/inview.ts`; they are the only kind allowed in a hero. **GSAP** effects (SplitLineReveal, WordStagger, ScribbleUnderline, OutlinedToSolid, ScrambleText, WeightShift) are marked `data-gsap` and driven by `src/effects/gsap-effects.ts` (GSAP 3 with ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVG and ScrambleText from npm, bundled by Astro into one lazy chunk). `src/effects/gsap-lazy.ts` imports that chunk only after first contentful paint and only when the first `data-gsap` element comes within one viewport, and skips any element inside `.hero`. Every effect: transform and opacity only (ScribbleUnderline animates one stroke dash, WeightShift one font axis, GradientSweep and the Keyword fill one background position, ScrambleText the characters of one aria-hidden copy: paint only, no layout), fires once on scroll into view, runs only when `html.motion` is set (Base.astro sets it when the visitor allows motion), and shows its finished state under `prefers-reduced-motion` and without JavaScript. The real sentence always stays one text node for screen readers and the audit.

## Harvest rule

After a client build passes `rigal-site-audit`, any new section or effect that is not already here gets stripped of client content, dropped into `src/sections/<archetype>/` or `src/effects/`, rendered on `/kit`, and given one line in the table above with its fingerprint. The next client starts with it.

## Google review styles (GR)

Say "use GR1", "GR2" or "GR3" to swap a client's reviews section. All three read the same list in `src/data/reviews.ts` (real Google reviews only, word for word; `highlight` marks exact phrases from the text) and the rating and count from `SITE.rating` and `SITE.reviewCount`. With no reviews each shows the "read them on Google" fallback; with a count of 0 no numbers show. Preview all three on `/kit` (the kit passes `sample` to show the rating block with bracketed placeholders). New styles are added as GR4, GR5 and so on, listed here and on `/kit`.
